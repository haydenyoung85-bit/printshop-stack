Slicers

Software that turns 3D models into printer instructions.

  • Bambu Studio

    Bambu Lab's official slicer, with project files, multi-plate support, and device control for Bambu printers..

  • OrcaSlicer

    Community open-source slicer forked from Bambu Studio, with calibration tools and profiles for many printer brands..

  • PrusaSlicer

    Prusa Research's open-source slicer, with profiles for Prusa and many third-party printers..

  • UltiMaker Cura

    UltiMaker's widely used slicer with a large library of printer profiles and plugins..
