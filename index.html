Tools for running a 3D print business

A small directory of software and suppliers for 3D print shops. Pricing comes from each vendor's official site (checked 2026-10-20). There are no ratings or reviews here, just descriptions.

Print-Farm Software

  • Printago

    Cloud print-farm management software that queues and dispatches jobs across many printers, including Bambu Lab machines.

  • SimplyPrint

    Browser-based printer management with remote control, print queues, cloud slicing, filament tracking and AI failure detection, across multiple printer brands..

  • OctoPrint

    Open-source web interface for controlling and monitoring a single 3D printer, usually run on a Raspberry Pi, with a large plugin ecosystem..

  • Obico

    AI-based print failure detection and remote access for OctoPrint and Klipper printers; open source, with a hosted cloud option or self-hosting..

  • 3DPrinterOS

    Cloud platform for managing fleets of 3D printers, aimed largely at schools, universities and enterprises..

  • AutoFarm3D (3DQue)

    Print-farm software from 3DQue for queueing, monitoring and failure detection, which can run on a Raspberry Pi 4 for local, cloud-free remote access..

Slicers

  • Bambu Studio

    Bambu Lab's official slicer, with project files, multi-plate support, and device control for Bambu printers..

  • OrcaSlicer

    Community open-source slicer forked from Bambu Studio, with calibration tools and profiles for many printer brands..

  • PrusaSlicer

    Prusa Research's open-source slicer, with profiles for Prusa and many third-party printers..

  • UltiMaker Cura

    UltiMaker's widely used slicer with a large library of printer profiles and plugins..

AI 3D Model Generators

  • Meshy

    AI generator that turns text prompts or images into 3D models, with texturing, and has a 3D printing use case..

  • Tripo

    AI 3D model generator from text or images, with retopology and texture tools..

  • Hyper3D Rodin

    Hyper3D's Rodin generator creates 3D models from one or more images or text..

Filament Brands

  • Bambu Lab Filament

    Bambu Lab's own filament line, including RFID-tagged spools that Bambu AMS units read automatically..

  • Polymaker

    Filament maker with a wide range, from PolyTerra/PolyLite PLA to engineering materials..

  • Elegoo Filament

    Elegoo sells budget-oriented PLA and other filaments alongside its printers..

  • eSUN

    Long-running filament manufacturer with PLA+, PETG and specialty materials..

  • Overture

    Filament brand sold direct and on marketplaces, known for PLA and PETG lines..

  • SUNLU

    Filament brand that also sells filament dryers..

Etsy & Shopify Selling Tools

  • Etsy

    Marketplace for handmade and custom goods; a common first sales channel for 3D print shops..

  • Shopify

    Hosted e-commerce platform for running your own online store..

  • eRank

    Etsy SEO and keyword research tool with listing audits and shop analytics..

  • Marmalead

    Etsy SEO and keyword research tool..

  • EverBee

    Etsy product research and analytics tool, with a Chrome extension and free calculators such as an Etsy fee calculator..
