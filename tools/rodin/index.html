Hyper3D Rodin

What it is

Hyper3D's Rodin generator creates 3D models from one or more images or text.

Who it's for

Creators and teams who want multi-image-to-3D and API access.

Pricing

Free (pay-per-credit, $1.50/credit). Creator $30/mo, Business $120/mo with API access (lower when billed yearly).
