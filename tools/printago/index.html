Printago

What it is

Cloud print-farm management software that queues and dispatches jobs across many printers, including Bambu Lab machines. Its pricing is based on production slots (throughput) rather than per printer.

Who it's for

Shops running several printers that want a central job queue and automated dispatch.

Pricing

First production slot free; additional slots are paid (amount not visible in page text when checked, see site). 14-day free trial.

Taken from the official site on 2026-10-20. Prices change, so confirm before you buy.

Pros

  • Pricing is tied to production slots, not printer count, per the vendor
  • Free first slot lets you try it on a real workload
  • Vendor states it works with Bambu Lab printers

Cons

  • Cloud-based, so it depends on an internet connection and a third-party service
  • Paid slot pricing was not readable without the interactive page
