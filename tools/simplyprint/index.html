SimplyPrint

Browser-based printer management with remote control, print queues, cloud slicing, filament tracking and AI failure detection, across multiple printer brands.

01

What it is

Browser-based printer management with remote control, print queues, cloud slicing, filament tracking and AI failure detection, across multiple printer brands.

02

Who it's for

Hobbyists up to print farms; the Print Farm tier targets businesses managing printers at scale.

03

Pricing

Free plan (2 printers). Basic $5.99/mo, Pro $9.99/mo, Print Farm $39.99/mo, Enterprise $299/mo (monthly billing; lower when billed yearly). Extra printers billed per printer.

Taken from the official site on 2026-10-20. Prices change, so confirm before you buy.

04

Pros and cons

+Pros

  • Free plan available
  • Tiered plans from hobby to farm
  • Includes cloud slicing and filament management per the pricing page

−Cons

  • AI failure detection is listed as a separate paid add-on
  • Costs scale with printer count

These notes summarize public vendor information. Print Shop Stack has not done hands-on testing.
