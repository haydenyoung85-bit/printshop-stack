AutoFarm3D (3DQue)

What it is

Print-farm software from 3DQue for queueing, monitoring and failure detection, which can run on a Raspberry Pi 4 for local, cloud-free remote access.

Who it's for

Farms wanting automated queuing; 3DQue also sells hardware for automatic part removal.

Pricing

Monthly: $39.99/mo plus $9.99/mo per printer add-on. Annual pricing also listed (see site).

Taken from the official site on 2026-10-20. Prices change, so confirm before you buy.
